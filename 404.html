Sunday

On Sunday, record it and watch the words appear.

Press record as the pastor begins. The sermon is written out on your phone as it's preached, with no internet needed in the sanctuary. Afterwards, Scribe gives you the summary, the outline and every verse that was read.

Tuesday

On Tuesday, bring it to your small group.

Your note, the verses and the discussion questions, ready to share as a link anyone can open. The recording and transcript always stay with you.

Wednesday

On Wednesday, ask what you've been wondering.

Ask about any passage, or describe the verse you half remember. Answers come with the verses, in the translation you read, and you can keep asking.

  • What does grace mean in Ephesians 2?
  • Where is the verse about the mustard seed?
  • Why are there four Gospels?
